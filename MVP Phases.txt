# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT — NEZTA CLIENT DEMO MVP

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Act as a senior full-stack JavaScript developer, product designer, UI/UX specialist and software architect.

Build a professional, interactive MVP of **NEZTA**, a multi-shop food ordering and delivery platform.

This version is specifically intended to be demonstrated to a prospective client so they can understand the product, experience its workflows and decide whether they want to proceed with full-scale development.

**This is a sales demonstration MVP, not the final production platform.**

The application must demonstrate the core business concept through realistic, working interactions.

Do not attempt to implement every feature from the full NEZTA roadmap.

Prioritize:
- Visual quality.
- Business workflow demonstration.
- Easy navigation.
- Realistic sample data.
- Functional food ordering.
- Customizable meal boxes.
- Tiffin subscriptions.
- Bulk and corporate orders.
- Shop operations.
- Kitchen management.
- Delivery management.
- Platform-level overview.

The final experience should make the client feel that they are looking at an actual software product that can be developed into a complete commercial platform.

- Frontend: Cloudflare Pages.
- Backend: Cloudflare Workers.
- Database: Neon PostgreSQL.

Do not introduce Redis, queues, payment gateway integration, WhatsApp integration or Google Maps unless a specific MVP feature genuinely requires them.

Keep the architecture compatible with the planned full NEZTA platform.

Use exactly two primary application directories:

- `client/`
- `server/`

Do not use TypeScript or TSX.

## 3. MVP SCOPE

Build seven connected experiences:

1. Customer Marketplace.
2. Shop Admin Dashboard.
3. Kitchen Management.
4. Rider Delivery Interface.
5. Platform Admin Dashboard.
6. Tiffin Subscription Management.
7. Bulk and Corporate Ordering.

Meal boxes are an important part of the customer marketplace and must support customization.

All experiences should work with the same sample business data and database.

For the demonstration, NEZTA is the first shop on the platform.

Use one active shop initially, but structure the database and API so additional shops can be introduced later.

Do not build a complicated multi-tenant onboarding system in the MVP.

However, every shop-owned record should have a `shop_id` where applicable. Avoid architectural decisions that would make future tenant isolation difficult.
